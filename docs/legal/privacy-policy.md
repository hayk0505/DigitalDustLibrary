# Privacy Policy — Digital Dust Library

*Last updated: September 2, 2026*

## What this covers

This policy explains what information Digital Dust Library
(digitaldustlibrary.com, the "Site") collects and how it's used. It applies
to visitors, and separately to contributor/author accounts.

## Information we collect

**If you apply to become an author:** name, email address, and whatever you
write in your pitch. Stored to process the application; if approved, your
name and email become part of your author account.

**If you're a reader:** we don't require an account or collect personal
information just to read the Site. Basic, aggregate analytics are
collected via Cloudflare Web Analytics (see below).

**If you're a logged-in contributor:** your name, email, and the content
you author, stored to run the editorial workflow (drafts, review, and
publishing).

## Analytics and tracking

The Site uses **Cloudflare Web Analytics**, deployed via **Google Tag
Manager (GTM)**. Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookieless by design — it
doesn't use tracking cookies or collect personally identifiable
information, and it doesn't track you across other websites. It measures
aggregate traffic (page views, load performance) only.

GTM itself is a tag container — right now it only loads the Cloudflare
Analytics beacon described above. If additional tags are ever added through
GTM in the future that do use cookies or collect personal data (e.g.,
advertising or more detailed analytics tools), this policy needs to be
updated first, and a cookie-consent mechanism may become necessary at that
point since the cookieless status described here would no longer hold.

## Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Cloudflare Web
Analytics, as deployed on this Site, does not use tracking cookies — see
"Analytics and tracking" above. If that changes in the future (for
example, if a new tag is added through GTM that does use cookies), this
section will be updated to explain what's set and how to control it
through your browser settings.

## Third-party services

- **Cloudflare** — hosting, DNS, and the analytics described above.
- **Brevo** — sends transactional email (e.g., author-application
  notifications, invite emails). Recipient email addresses are shared with
  Brevo solely to send these emails.

## International data transfers

Some of the service providers above may process data on servers located
outside Armenia, including in the United States and the European Union.

## How we use information

- To process author applications and run contributor accounts.
- To understand aggregate site traffic and improve the Site.
- We don't sell personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

## Data retention

Author application data is retained for as long as your application is
under review. If your application isn't approved, or if an approved
contributor account later becomes inactive, related personal data is
deleted or anonymized within 3 years of your last interaction with the
Site — sooner on request (see "Your rights" below). Active contributor
accounts retain the data needed to run the account for as long as the
account stays active.

## Your rights

If you're in the EU/EEA (or another jurisdiction with similar protections),
you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and
to object to certain processing. Contact us using the details below to
make a request.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the Site's tracking or data practices change.
Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
